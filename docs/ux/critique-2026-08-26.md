Here is the prioritized--- UI/UX critique. Your most severe problems exist on the public side, directly sabotaging conversions and triggering the manual chasing you want to avoid.
Critical Flow Failures (Highest Impact)

1. The Buried and Fragmented Signup (Public) This is your biggest failure. It actively prevents Goal 1. On mobile, the only "Sign up" button is buried thousands of pixels down the page. Furthermore, the three vital steps (register, waiver, pay) are disjointed, relying on players reading instructions to complete the process. Change: Make the call-to-action sticky on mobile event pages. Consolidate the steps into an unavoidable, single-flow wizard.
2. Unusable Match-Night Roster (Admin) This destroys Goal 2. Outdoors on a phone, the roster table forces horizontal scrolling just to view waiver or payment statuses. Tap targets (like 11x11px buttons) are impossibly small for a standing, moving user. Change: Abandon tabular data on mobile screens. Use stacked player cards showing all statuses at a glance, with minimum 44x44px touch targets.
3. Missing Spanish Support (Public) Your heavily bilingual audience is forced to navigate an English-only flow, causing drop-offs and manual WhatsApp inquiries. Change: Implement a prominent Spanish language toggle for the entire conversion path.

Mobile Layout & Usability Obstacles

4. The Tablet Overflow Break (Both) At exactly 768px, desktop navigation elements render prematurely, causing up to 113px of horizontal overflow and breaking the layout sideways. Change: Adjust breakpoint triggers to retain the mobile menu until horizontal space naturally accommodates the desktop navigation.
5. Hidden Admin Navigation (Admin) Over 150px of the mobile admin navigation is pushed off-screen, completely hiding the sign-out control. Change: Consolidate the admin header into a standard mobile menu.
6. Broken Edge Cases (Public) The direct `/pay` page claims users can "pick an event below" but displays nothing. Additionally, the homepage's fixed bottom bar overlaps and hides footer links. Change: Populate the `/pay` fallback state and add proper bottom padding to the homepage container.

Trust, Accessibility & Jargon

7. Low Contrast & Small Targets (Both) Muted text falls to a 3.5:1 contrast, failing accessibility—especially for players reading outdoors in the glare. The admin also features 85+ tiny interactive elements. Change: Ensure all text meets a 4.5:1 contrast ratio and enforce a 44px minimum hit area globally.
8. Developer Jargon (Admin) The diagnostics page exposes your non-technical owner to terms like "JWT" and "Supabase auth," causing unnecessary friction. Change: Translate this into a simple green/red system-status dashboard.
9. Contradictory Copy (Public) The facility page advertises "turf" while event pages advertise "grass." Change: Unify your physical facility descriptions to build trust.
